How the 18% split works

Every completed job is split automatically. The platform fee is exactly 18% — the handyman receives the remaining 82%, paid out to their connected account.

Frequently asked questions

When am I charged as a client?

You pay when you confirm a booking. The payment is held securely and the job is marked complete once the work is done — then the handyman is paid automatically.

How much do handymen actually receive?

Exactly 82% of the job total. For example, on a $250 job the platform fee is $45 (18%) and the handyman receives $205 (82%).

Are there any subscriptions or listing fees?

No. Creating an account, listing your services and booking handymen are all free. The only fee is the 18% commission on completed jobs.

How do handymen get paid?

Through Stripe Connect. Each handyman connects a payout account during onboarding, and earnings are transferred automatically after each completed job.

What if a job is cancelled?

Cancelled jobs are never charged the commission. Pending jobs show the expected payout, but the 18% fee only applies once the job is completed and paid.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes — use the “Continue with Google” button on the sign-in screen to create or access your account in one click.
