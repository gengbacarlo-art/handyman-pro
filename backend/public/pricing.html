Questions, answered

Who pays the 18% platform fee?

The fee is deducted from the contract amount when a milestone is released. The client pays the agreed price, and the handyman receives 82% of the milestone. There is no separate charge added on top for clients.

When am I charged?

Clients fund a milestone when work is about to begin. The money sits in escrow and is released only after the client approves the submitted work. Posting jobs, receiving proposals and messaging are always free.

Are there connects, credits or subscriptions?

No. Handyman Pro uses a single flat 18% fee per released milestone. Handymen can send unlimited proposals and clients can post unlimited jobs without buying credits.

How does escrow work?

The client funds a milestone → the handyman does the work and submits it → the client approves (or requests changes) → payment is released 82% to the handyman and 18% to the platform. If changes are requested, the milestone stays funded while the work is revised.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Use “Request changes” on the submitted milestone. The payment stays in escrow and the handyman can revise and resubmit. In a production deployment, unresolved disputes would be escalated to platform support.

Is this handling real money?

This build runs in demo mode: funding, releases and payouts are simulated so you can test the full workflow safely. The data model is Stripe Connect ready — see the README for the production wiring.

Can I cancel a job or contract?

You can cancel a job while it is still open and has no contract. Once a contract is active, the parties should resolve it through milestones, change requests and messaging. Completed contracts remain in your history.
