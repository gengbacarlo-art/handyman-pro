How it works

Hiring, minus the guesswork

A transparent marketplace built around proposals, contracts and milestones — so both sides know exactly what happens next.

For clients

Get your project done

1
Post your job

Describe the work, set a budget and timeline. Posting is always free.

2
Compare proposals

Review bids, ratings, experience and cover letters side by side.

3
Hire and fund a milestone

Your payment is held in escrow — the pro knows the money is there.

4
Approve and release

Pay only when you're happy with the work, then leave a review.

For handymen

Build a business you own

1
Create your profile

Show your trade, skills, rate, experience and availability.

2
Send proposals

Bid your price and timeline on jobs that fit your skills.

3
Do great work

Message the client, share updates and submit completed milestones.

4
Keep 82%

Get paid when the client approves. Build reviews that win better jobs.
