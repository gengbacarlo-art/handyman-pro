FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about fees, escrow and getting started. Can't find your answer? We're happy to help.

Contact us
Who pays the 18% fee?

The fee is deducted from the contract amount when a milestone is released. The handyman receives 82%; the client pays the agreed contract amount with no added platform fee.

When am I charged?

Posting a job, sending proposals and hiring are free. The client funds a milestone before work starts. The money is held in escrow and released only when the client approves the completed work.

Are there connects, credits or subscriptions?

No. There are no connects, credits, monthly subscriptions or hidden charges. The only platform fee is the flat 18% on each released milestone.

How does escrow work?

The client funds a milestone, the handyman completes and submits the work, and the client approves or requests changes. On approval, 82% is released to the handyman and 18% is retained by the platform.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Request changes from the contract page. The milestone stays funded while the handyman revises the work. If you still can't agree, contact support for help resolving the contract.

Is this real money?

Not in this demo. Funding, releases and payouts are simulated so you can walk through the full workflow. The data model is Stripe Connect-ready for real escrow payments.

Can I cancel a job or contract?

Open jobs can be cancelled before a contract starts. Once work is underway, use milestones and change requests to adjust the scope. Completed contracts stay in your history with their reviews.
